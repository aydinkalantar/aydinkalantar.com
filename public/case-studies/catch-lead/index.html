Catch Lead

A sales agent that never misses a visitor.

Visit catchlead.dev ↗
Role
Product design & development
Type
B2B SaaS
Platform
Website widget + PWA
Live
catchlead.dev

01  Overview

Catch Lead adds an AI assistant to a service business's website. It answers visitors by chat or voice, in their language, quotes from the business's own prices, books appointments and emails the owner every lead.

02  Challenges

  • Missed calls are lost jobs. Plumbers, roofers, HVAC teams and law firms lose customers after hours and while they are out on jobs.
  • Owners are not technical. They have no time for scripts, integrations or training a bot, so setup had to take minutes.
  • A wrong price is worse than no answer. An assistant that invents a quote damages the business, so every answer had to come from the business itself.
  • One script on someone else's website. The widget has to look right and work on any site (Wix, WordPress, Squarespace or custom code) without breaking the page around it.

03  UI/UX solutions

I treated setup as the product and trust as the constraint, and designed for two different moments: the visitor, who should feel like they are on a phone call, and the owner, who checks leads on a phone between jobs.

Onboarding in three steps

Paste the website address, add one line of code, and leads start arriving. Before signing up, an owner can enter their URL and see the assistant on a preview of their own site in seconds.

The orb

A single glowing orb invites visitors to talk or type. They can interrupt naturally, as on a call, send photos of the job, and switch to text at any time. Max greets them with a short card first, so the voice never starts without a tap.

A handoff that feels personal

When a visitor needs a person, the assistant collects their name, contact details and needs in the conversation itself, then tells them the owner will follow up.

A dashboard built for the truck

Leads, conversations and bookings live in one installable app with a swipeable weekly calendar. Each lead arrives already written up with a summary and quote. A quote becomes an invoice in one tap, and marking a job done sends it.

The agent

Meet Max: a greeting, then a conversation.

The orb waits quietly at the foot of the page. A short greeting offers two ways in, typing or talking, and the panel opens into a chat with quick replies for the questions every business gets.

On a client's site

One tap, and Max picks up.

Running live for a Los Angeles apparel printer. A single button sits on the page; visitors talk or type, Max remembers returning customers, takes the details, and asks for a rating when the conversation ends.

Try it here: the assistant in the corner of this site is Catch Lead.

The dashboard

Every lead arrives already written up.

The owner's side is a phone-first dashboard in light and dark mode. Each conversation becomes a lead with an AI summary, the exact request and a priced quote, ready to turn into an invoice or a booked visit.

In motion

Explained in forty seconds, sold in ten.

An explainer for the website and a set of short vertical films for social, each built around the same moment: a customer reaching out after hours and getting an answer instead of silence.

04  Technical solutions

One script tag, fully isolated

The widget loads from a single script and renders inside a closed Shadow DOM, so the host site's styles cannot break it and the widget cannot leak styles into the page. It follows the site's light or dark theme and is configured through attributes on the script tag.

Real-time voice

Voice runs over one streaming WebSocket connection to Google's Gemini Live, which gives a natural voice and lets visitors interrupt mid-sentence. The assistant detects the visitor's language across 13 options and offers 30 voices.

Grounded answers

At setup Catch Lead reads the business's website for services, prices and service area. The assistant answers only from that knowledge, which the owner can edit, and quotes only published prices.

Leads, bookings and payments

Every conversation is turned into a lead with a summary and quote, and the owner is emailed right away. Bookings land in the calendar, Stripe handles deposits, and the same inbox now covers Instagram, WhatsApp and SMS. Usage alerts at 80% and 100% replace hard limits, so a lead is never blocked.

05  Outcome

Catch Lead is live with a 14-day free trial that needs no card.

  • Plans run from solo operators to agencies, with white-label and multi-location support at the top tier.
  • Leads are never hard-blocked at a plan limit; owners get usage alerts at 80% and 100% instead.
  • The product grew into an omnichannel inbox for Instagram, WhatsApp and SMS, with Stripe deposits and review automation.
