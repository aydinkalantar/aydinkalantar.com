Questions

About automation.

What can be automated in a small business?+

Usually more than people expect: replying to new leads, sending quotes, booking appointments, moving orders between systems, invoicing, reminders and weekly reports. We start with the task that costs you the most time.

Do I have to change the software I use?+

Usually not. Most automation connects the tools you already have so they share information without anyone copying it by hand.

Where does AI fit in?+

AI handles the work that needs reading or writing: answering customer questions, sorting incoming requests, pulling details from documents and drafting replies for you to approve.

Have you built automation before?+

Yes. Catch Lead automates lead capture, quoting and booking for service businesses, and Auto Alpha runs trading strategies automatically on users' own exchange accounts.
