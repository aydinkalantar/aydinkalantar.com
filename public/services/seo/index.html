Questions

About search.

What is local SEO?+

Local SEO helps your business appear when people nearby search for what you do, such as "web designer near me", in the Google map pack and local results. Much of it rests on a complete Google Business Profile, consistent listings and reviews.

What is AI search optimization?+

More customers now ask ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews or Perplexity instead of searching. AI search optimization makes your site easy for those assistants to read, trust and quote: clear facts, structured data, crawler access and pages that answer real questions.

How long does SEO take?+

Technical fixes can show results within weeks. Rankings for competitive searches usually take a few months of steady work. Paid ads can bring traffic from the first day while SEO builds.

Have you done SEO before?+

Yes. From 2017 to 2020 I ran SEO, Google Ads and Facebook Ads for The Best Screen Printing and Signs Market in Los Angeles, and in 2026 I moved The Best Screen Printing to a new site with its search traffic intact.

Do you manage Google Ads and Facebook Ads?+

Yes. I set up campaigns, tracking and landing pages, and report on what each dollar brought in.
