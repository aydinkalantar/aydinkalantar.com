Questions

About websites.

How much does a website cost?+

It depends on the number of pages and features, such as online ordering or booking. After a short call you get the scope and price in writing, so you know what you are paying for before anything starts.

Will I lose my Google rankings if I redesign my site?+

Not if the move is planned. On The Best Screen Printing rebuild, every one of 2,662 old addresses was kept, redirected or retired on purpose, and the pages that were kept carry 96% of the clicks the old site earned.

Can I edit the site myself?+

Yes. I set the site up so the things you change often, like prices, products or photos, are easy to update, and I show you how.

Do you work with businesses outside Cleveland?+

Yes. I am based in Mayfield Heights and happy to meet locally, and I work remotely with clients anywhere in the United States.

Do you use WordPress or templates?+

I usually build custom, lightweight sites because they load faster and are more secure. If WordPress or Shopify is the right fit for your team, I work with those too.
