Questions

About pricing.

Are there any hidden fees?+

No. After a short call you get the scope, timeline and price in writing before any work starts. Ad spend, if you run ads, is paid directly to Google or Meta.

Is there a contract?+

Monthly plans have a 3-month minimum, then continue month to month.

How is the setup fee paid?+

Half to start and half at launch.

Why does a done-for-you AI agent cost more than a $79 app?+

The app is only the software. I set it up, train it on your services and prices, connect it to your phone, calendar and CRM, and check every month that it answers well and books jobs. You get the result without learning another tool.

Do you meet in person?+

Yes. I am based in Mayfield Heights and meet clients in person across Cleveland and Northeast Ohio. I also work remotely with businesses anywhere in the United States.

What if I need something that is not listed?+

Email or call with a few lines about what you need. Most projects mix a few services, and I quote them together.
