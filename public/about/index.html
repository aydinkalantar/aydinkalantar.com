About

Designer, developer, maker.

I'm Aydin Kalantar, a product designer based in Mayfield Heights, Ohio. I take products from first sketch to shipped interface, and I build the code behind them. Before any of that, I learned precision at a workbench, cutting natural wood veneer by hand.

Story

My work sits where digital systems meet physical craft.

Marquetry taught me to see a whole picture as hundreds of small parts, each cut to fit its neighbors. A portrait in veneer only works when every fragment respects the grain, the tone and the line next to it. There is no undo.

I bring the same discipline to software. A product is a system of small decisions, from a button's state to a pricing rule, and it only feels effortless when each one fits. I design those decisions, then build them, so nothing gets lost between the idea and what ships.

Along the way I worked in graphic design and in 3D modeling and rendering. That is where I learned composition, light and material, and it still shapes how I present every product.

Today that practice runs in parallel: three live products on one side, original veneer artwork on the other.

My family name appears in a few spellings, so you may also find me as Aydin Kalantarov, Aydin Kalantarli or Aydin Kalan.

Principles

  1. 01

    Respect the material.

    Wood has grain; data has shape; people have habits. Good work follows them instead of fighting them.

  2. 02

    Measure twice.

    Understand the problem before cutting anything. A clear plan is cheaper than a clever fix.

  3. 03

    Be honest in the interface.

    Show the risk beside the reward, the price before the commitment and the state of every action.

  4. 04

    Finish the edges.

    Empty states, errors and small screens are where craft shows. They get the same care as the hero.
