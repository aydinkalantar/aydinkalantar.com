Questions

About branding.

What do I get with a logo project?+

Final logo files for print and screen, color and black-and-white versions, and a short guide to the colors and fonts so your brand stays consistent.

Can you design for screen printing and embroidery?+

Yes. I learned screen printing end to end and ran apparel decoration work, so artwork comes ready for production, with the right colors and separations.

What is your design background?+

I studied oil painting at the Azerbaijan Fine Art College, worked in my father's printing company in Baku, and from 2017 to 2020 led branding for The Best Screen Printing and Signs Market in Los Angeles.

Can you also build the website?+

Yes. Brand, website and marketing can come from one person, which keeps everything consistent.
