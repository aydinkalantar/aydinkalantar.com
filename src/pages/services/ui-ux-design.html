Questions

About UI & UX.

What is the difference between UI and UX design?+

UX design is how a product works: what a user needs, the steps they take and where they get stuck. UI design is how it looks and feels on screen: layout, type, color and every button state. I do both, and they work best together.

Can you hand designs to my developers?+

Yes. I hand over organized design files with every screen, state and spacing rule, or I build the interface myself so nothing is lost between design and code.

Can you improve an app or site that already exists?+

Yes. A UX audit finds where users stall or leave, then I redesign those screens first, so you see results without a full rebuild.

What have you designed?+

Virtual Fashion, Catch Lead and Auto Alpha: an AI design studio, a chat and voice sales agent with an owner dashboard, and a trading dashboard built to make complex data easy to read. All three are live.

Do you also design for mobile?+

Yes. Every design covers phone, tablet and desktop, and the apps I build install on mobile.
