Questions

About apps.

Can you build an app from scratch?+

Yes. Virtual Fashion, Catch Lead and Auto Alpha are SaaS products I designed and built from the first sketch to live, paying-ready software. Each is online today.

Do you build mobile apps?+

Yes. I build mobile apps and installable web apps that work on phones, tablets and desktop. Virtual Fashion and Catch Lead both run as installable apps on mobile.

How long does it take to build a first version?+

It depends on the features. Most products start with a focused first version that real users can try, then grow from what they do. You get the scope, timeline and price in writing before work starts.

Will I own the code?+

Yes. The code, designs and accounts are yours.

Can you add AI to my product?+

Yes. All three of my products use AI: image and video generation in Virtual Fashion, chat and voice in Catch Lead, and strategy automation in Auto Alpha.
