Questions

About AI agents.

What is an AI website agent?+

It is an assistant on your website that answers visitors in plain language, by typing or by voice. Unlike a scripted chatbot, it understands questions in the visitor's own words and answers from your real information.

Will it make things up?+

It answers from the material you approve, such as your services, prices and policies. When it does not know, it says so and takes the visitor's details so you can follow up.

Can I try one before buying?+

Yes. The assistant in the corner of this page is Catch Lead, the product I built. Ask it anything about my work.

Does it work with my current website?+

Usually yes. It is added with one line of code and works on WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace and custom sites.

Can it take orders by voice?+

Yes. On The Best Screen Printing site, Max takes apparel orders by voice, remembers returning customers and asks for a rating at the end.
