Questions

About 3D work.

What do you need to start a product render?+

Photos, drawings or measurements of the product, plus logos and colors. A physical sample helps but is not required.

Do you make product animations?+

Yes. Short loops and launch videos for ads, social and product pages.

What 3D experience do you have?+

I began with 3D modeling, animation and rendering for film and television in Baku, including CGI for The Temple's Heart in 2005, and now build Virtual Fashion, an AI studio for apparel imagery.

Can you produce on-model apparel images without a photo shoot?+

Yes. Virtual Fashion, the studio I built, places garments with your design on consistent models and exports photos and video.
