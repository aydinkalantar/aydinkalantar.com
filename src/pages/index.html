The craft — Natural wood veneer

Made by hand, one sheet at a time.

Long before interfaces, I worked in wood. Each marquetry piece is assembled from hundreds of hand-cut fragments of natural veneer, chosen for grain, tone and the way it catches light. Nothing is stained or printed; every color is the wood's own.

The discipline carries into everything I design: respect for the material, tolerance measured in fractions of a millimeter, and the patience to fit each part until the whole feels inevitable.
