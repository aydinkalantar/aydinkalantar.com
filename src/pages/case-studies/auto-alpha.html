01  Overview

Auto Alpha runs rule-based crypto strategies on a user's own exchange account, around the clock. Funds never leave the exchange; Auto Alpha only places trades.

I designed and built the whole product: the strategies and their testing, the trading engine, the dashboard, and the voiced explainer films that walk people through it before they connect any money.

02  Challenges

  • Trust before money. People are asked to connect real money to a system they cannot see inside.
  • Products that hide the risk. Automated trading tools tend to lead with their best return and bury the losses.
  • Dense data for non-experts. Equity curves, drawdowns and trade logs had to be readable by people who are not quants.
  • Security of exchange keys. The product places trades on the user's own exchange account, so a leaked key or forged signal must never be able to move funds.

03  UI/UX solutions

I set a few rules before designing any screen: show the drop next to the gain, label every hypothetical, show every trade (losses included), and make stopping easier than starting.

Strategy cards

Each card puts backtest return, the period covered, risk level, worst drop and the simulated record since launch in one compact frame, so strategies can be compared honestly at a glance. Detail pages add a log-scale equity curve and an every-trade ledger.

Shown, then explained

A one-minute film on the home page walks through the whole setup with demo data, and each strategy has its own film that explains its rules in plain words before anyone allocates to it.

Practice first

Every account starts with $10,000 of paper money at live prices, so people can watch a strategy behave before risking anything.

A guided, careful connection

Connecting an exchange is a step-by-step flow that explains each setting, such as why the key must be trade-only, before anything is saved.

Stop controls in reach

Users can pause new entries, let open trades close, or stop everything at market in one action, from the desktop or a phone.

Pricing that explains itself

A calculator compares the profit-share plan with the flat monthly plan for the user's own numbers and shows where one becomes cheaper than the other.

The bar for a strategy

Eight new strategy ideas tested in September. None made the cut.

A strategy is designed on Bitcoin from 2018 to 2023, then judged only on what it never saw: 2024 onward, and ten other coins with the same settings. It also has to pass four stress tests. Most ideas fail, and the ones that fail are not published.

Strategy explainers

Every strategy, explained before anyone starts it.

Each strategy family has its own voiced film on its page: the idea in one line, when it buys and sells, what the Long and Short version adds, the safety stop, and its backtest, labelled hypothetical on screen and in the voiceover.

On the live site

Pick one. Every number labelled.

The home page says what Auto Alpha does and what it costs in one paragraph. Strategy cards lead with this year's backtest, but the hypothetical label, the worst drop and the strategy's own simulated record since launch sit right beside it, and the disclaimer comes before any number.

04  Technical solutions

Funds never leave the exchange

Users create a trade-only API key locked to Auto Alpha's IP address. Keys with withdrawal rights are refused, stored keys are encrypted with AES-256, and two-factor sign-in is required before any real-money trade.

Signals that can't be forged or replayed

Incoming TradingView webhooks are validated and protected against replay before any order goes out, and signals that arrive late are dropped rather than chased.

Risk limits at the exchange

Stop-losses are placed on the exchange itself, so they hold even if Auto Alpha is offline, and leverage is capped at 1×.

Honest testing

The backtest replays the same code that trades live, candle by candle from January 2018, deciding on a closed candle and filling at the next open, with fees and slippage on every entry and exit. Every active strategy is re-tested on the 1st of each month. Backtest, simulated and real-money figures are stored and shown separately, never mixed.

An exchange earns real money

Each exchange has to complete a full testnet run (entries, strategy exits, stops and reconciliation against the exchange's own records) before it accepts real-money keys. Until then it stays listed for paper and testnet only.

Reporting

Paper accounts run at live prices, weekly email reports summarize activity, and real-money trades export to CSV for the tax year.

05  Outcome

Auto Alpha is live for real money on Binance (spot and futures) and Coinbase (spot), with more exchanges on testnet.

  • Six strategies in ten versions, each with a stress-test badge and a public record of every signal since launch, and voiced explainers for the main families.
  • Private TradingView strategies on Pro.
  • Weekly email reports and yearly CSV export of real-money trades.
  • Risk disclosures written in plain language and linked from every strategy.

Trading involves risk of loss. Backtests and simulated records are hypothetical and do not reflect real-money results.
