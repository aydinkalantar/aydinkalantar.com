Profile

Product designer and full-stack engineer working where digital systems meet physical craft. I take products from first sketch to shipped interface and build the code behind them, so nothing gets lost between the idea and what ships. Today I design and build SaaS platforms, web and mobile apps, AI chat and voice agents and business automation, and run three live products of my own: Virtual Fashion, Catch Lead and Auto Alpha.

My path runs from oil painting and 3D animation for film in Baku, through brand, web and growth work for Los Angeles businesses, to product engineering with AI agents. Alongside it I make original natural wood veneer marquetry, cut and fitted entirely by hand. Marquetry taught me to see a whole picture as hundreds of small parts, each cut to fit its neighbors, and I bring that discipline to software.
